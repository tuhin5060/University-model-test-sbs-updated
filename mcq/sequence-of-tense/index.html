🔁 Sequence of Tense MCQ — উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যাসহ

Reported speech ও সময়ের মিল · মোট ৫০টি প্রশ্ন। প্রতিটি প্রশ্নের নিচে “উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা” চাপলে সঠিক উত্তর আর বাংলা ব্যাখ্যা দেখাবে।

💡

Reporting verb ও ঘটনার সময়ের সম্পর্ক দেখে tense নির্বাচন করো; সব ক্ষেত্রে tense বদলায় না।

⏱️ Sequence of Tense Model Test দাও 🎮 Fun Quiz

  1. ১. He said that he ____ busy.

    1. is
    2. was
    3. will be
    4. has been
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. was

    A past reporting verb usually backshifts present to past when reporting a past viewpoint.

    Reporting verb said অতীতে থাকলে reported অংশের present (is) সাধারণত past হয়: was।

  2. ২. The teacher said that man ____ mortal.

    1. was
    2. is
    3. had been
    4. will be
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. is

    Universal truths remain in the present even after a past reporting verb.

    মানুষ মরণশীল—চিরন্তন সত্য; reporting verb past হলেও present থাকে: is।

  3. ৩. The teacher said that Bangladesh ____ independent in 1971.

    1. has become
    2. becomes
    3. became
    4. had become
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. became

    A historical fact is expressed in past simple, not past perfect solely due to reporting.

    ১৯৭১-এর ঐতিহাসিক ঘটনা past simple-এ থাকে; শুধু said থাকার জন্য had become হয় না।

  4. ৪. He told me that he ____ in Khulna the previous year.

    1. had been working
    2. has been working
    3. has worked
    4. is working
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: A. had been working

    Past reporting + an earlier past period calls for past perfect continuous in this context.

    Previous year-এ কাজ চলছিল, আর বলা হয়েছে তারও পরে—আগের অতীতে চলমান কাজে past perfect continuous।

    উৎস: DU C Unit 2014–15

  5. ৫. Nobody had known before the presentation that Salma ____ the award.

    1. will receive
    2. would receive
    3. receives
    4. has received
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. would receive

    A future event viewed from a past point commonly uses would.

    অতীতের দৃষ্টিকোণ থেকে পরের (ভবিষ্যৎ) ঘটনা বোঝাতে will-এর বদলে would: would receive।

    উৎস: CU E Unit 2013–14

  6. ৬. I will meet you when she ____.

    1. will call
    2. called
    3. calls
    4. would call
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. calls

    Future main clause + when clause: present simple in the when clause.

    Main clause ভবিষ্যৎ (will meet); when-clause-এ will না বসে present simple: calls।

  7. ৭. He thought that he ____ the glass.

    1. breaks
    2. has broken
    3. had broken
    4. will break
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. had broken

    The breaking preceded the past thought: past perfect.

    ভাবনার আগেই গ্লাস ভেঙেছে—আগের অতীত কাজে past perfect: had broken।

  8. ৮. He talks as if he ____ mad.

    1. is
    2. were
    3. has been
    4. will be
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. were

    As if with a present main clause and unreal comparison can take past subjunctive were.

    As if দিয়ে অবাস্তব তুলনা (সে আসলে পাগল নয়)—present main clause-এর পরে were।

  9. ৯. He talked as though he ____ a ghost.

    1. sees
    2. has seen
    3. had seen
    4. will see
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. had seen

    Past main clause + unreal prior action after as though takes past perfect.

    Talked (past) + as though + অবাস্তব আগের ঘটনা → past perfect: had seen।

  10. ১০. It was ten years since we ____.

    1. met
    2. have met
    3. had met
    4. meet
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. had met

    Past main clause + since clause often uses past perfect for the earlier event.

    Main clause past (It was ten years since) হলে since-এর পরের আগের ঘটনায় past perfect: had met।

  11. ১১. I know when he usually ____.

    1. will arrive
    2. arrives
    3. arrived
    4. had arrived
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. arrives

    With a present main clause, a habitual action takes present simple: arrives.

    Main clause present (I know); সে সাধারণত কখন আসে—অভ্যাস বোঝাতে present simple: arrives।

  12. ১২. He said, “I will go.” Reported speech: He said that he ____.

    1. will go
    2. would go
    3. goes
    4. had gone
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. would go

    Will generally backshifts to would in reported speech after said.

    Said (past) থাকলে direct speech-এর will reported speech-এ would হয়।

  13. ১৩. She said that she ____ the work the day before.

    1. finishes
    2. has finished
    3. had finished
    4. will finish
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. had finished

    The work was completed before the past reporting time: past perfect.

    কাজটি বলার আগের দিন শেষ হয়েছিল—আগের অতীতে past perfect: had finished; the day before।

  14. ১৪. I will call you before I ____ for the airport.

    1. will leave
    2. left
    3. leave
    4. would leave
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. leave

    Before-clause referring to the future uses present simple.

    Before দিয়ে শুরু ভবিষ্যৎ clause-এ will বসে না, present simple: leave।

  15. ১৫. He said that the Earth ____ around the Sun.

    1. moved
    2. moves
    3. had moved
    4. will move
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. moves

    Universal truth remains present in reported speech.

    পৃথিবী সূর্যের চারদিকে ঘোরে—চিরন্তন সত্য, reported speech-এও present: moves।

  16. ১৬. No sooner had the teacher entered the room ____ the students stood up.

    1. when
    2. than
    3. then
    4. while
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. than

    Fixed correlative pattern: no sooner … than.

    No sooner ... than নির্দিষ্ট জোড়া; এখানে when বা then বসে না।

  17. ১৭. Hardly had I finished the work ____ he arrived.

    1. than
    2. while
    3. when
    4. since
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. when

    Fixed pattern: hardly … when (or before).

    Hardly/Scarcely ... when (বা before) নির্দিষ্ট জোড়া; than বসে না।

  18. ১৮. He left the room after he ____ the letter.

    1. writes
    2. has written
    3. had written
    4. will write
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. had written

    The letter-writing happened before he left; past perfect marks the earlier action.

    চিঠি লেখা আগে শেষ, তারপর ঘর ছেড়েছে—আগের কাজে past perfect: had written।

  19. ১৯. I reached the station after the train ____.

    1. left
    2. has left
    3. had left
    4. leaves
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. had left

    The train left before the speaker reached the station.

    আমি পৌঁছানোর আগেই ট্রেন চলে গিয়েছিল—আগের অতীত কাজে past perfect: had left।

  20. ২০. She promised that she ____ help us.

    1. will
    2. would
    3. shall
    4. can
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. would

    A past promise reporting a future action takes would.

    Promised (past) থাকায় ভবিষ্যতের প্রতিশ্রুতিতে will হয় would।

  21. ২১. He said that he ____ come if he had time.

    1. will
    2. would
    3. will have
    4. comes
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. would

    Reported conditional intention commonly uses would.

    Said (past) + শর্তসাপেক্ষ ইচ্ছা: would come if he had time।

  22. ২২. If he ____ tomorrow, I will tell him.

    1. will come
    2. came
    3. comes
    4. would come
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. comes

    First conditional uses present simple in the if-clause.

    First conditional: if-clause-এ present simple (comes), main clause-এ will।

  23. ২৩. She asked me where I ____.

    1. live
    2. do I live
    3. lived
    4. will live
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. lived

    Reported questions use statement word order and commonly backshift after asked.

    Reported question-এ প্রশ্নের order থাকে না (do I live নয়) এবং asked past হওয়ায় lived।

  24. ২৪. He asked whether I ____ the film.

    1. have seen
    2. had seen
    3. will see
    4. am seeing
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. had seen

    Reported yes/no questions commonly backshift present perfect to past perfect.

    Asked whether (past) থাকলে present perfect হয় past perfect: had seen।

  25. ২৫. The principal ordered that every student ____ present.

    1. is
    2. be
    3. was
    4. will be
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. be

    After order + that, the mandative subjunctive base form be is standard.

    Order that-এর পরে mandative subjunctive: every student be present (is নয়)।

  26. ২৬. He said, “I can swim.” Reported speech: He said that he ____ swim.

    1. can
    2. could
    3. will
    4. has
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. could

    Can commonly backshifts to could in reported speech.

    Said (past) থাকলে can হয় could: He said that he could swim।

  27. ২৭. She said, “I have finished.” Reported speech: She said that she ____ finished.

    1. has
    2. had
    3. will have
    4. is
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. had

    Present perfect commonly backshifts to past perfect after a past reporting verb.

    Present perfect (have finished) reported speech-এ past perfect হয়: had finished।

  28. ২৮. He said, “I saw him yesterday.” He said that he ____ him the day before.

    1. saw
    2. has seen
    3. had seen
    4. sees
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. had seen

    Past simple can backshift to past perfect in reported speech; yesterday becomes the day before.

    Saw → had seen, আর yesterday → the day before—reported speech-এর নিয়ম।

  29. ২৯. I know that he ____ the answer yesterday.

    1. knows
    2. knew
    3. will know
    4. has known
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. knew

    The subordinate event has a specified past time, so use past simple.

    Main clause present হলেও subordinate clause-এ নির্দিষ্ট অতীত সময় (yesterday) থাকায় past simple: knew।

  30. ৩০. She will succeed if she ____ hard.

    1. will work
    2. works
    3. worked
    4. would work
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. works

    Future main clause does not require will in the if-clause.

    If-clause-এ ভবিষ্যৎ বোঝাতেও will বসে না; she-এর সঙ্গে present simple: works।

  31. ৩১. He behaves as if he ____ everything.

    1. knows
    2. knew
    3. has known
    4. will know
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. knew

    As if for an unreal comparison can take past simple.

    Present main clause (behaves) + as if + অবাস্তব তুলনা → past simple: knew।

  32. ৩২. He behaved as if he ____ everything.

    1. knows
    2. knew
    3. had known
    4. will know
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. had known

    With a past main verb, an unreal prior state can take past perfect.

    Past main clause (behaved) + as if + অবাস্তব তুলনা → past perfect: had known।

  33. ৩৩. It is five years since I ____ you last.

    1. have met
    2. meet
    3. met
    4. had met
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. met

    It is + duration + since + past simple.

    It is + সময়কাল + since-এর পরে past simple: met।

  34. ৩৪. Five years ____ since I met you last.

    1. has passed
    2. have passed
    3. passed
    4. is passing
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. have passed

    When the duration phrase is the subject (five years), use present perfect plural.

    Five years subject হলে plural verb এবং present perfect: have passed।

  35. ৩৫. Since it ____ raining, we stayed at home.

    1. is
    2. was
    3. has
    4. will be
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. was

    Since meaning because takes a tense that matches the reason; past context uses was.

    এখানে since মানে because; ঘটনা অতীতের (we stayed), তাই was raining।

  36. ৩৬. He said that he would go. The word “would” ____ in reported speech.

    1. must change to went
    2. is already appropriate
    3. must change to gone
    4. must change to will
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. is already appropriate

    Would is retained as the backshifted form of will.

    Will-এর reported form-ই would; তাই would আর বদলাতে হয় না।

  37. ৩৭. I shall meet my friend when he ____.

    1. will come
    2. came
    3. comes
    4. will have come
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. comes

    Future-time when clause uses present simple.

    ভবিষ্যৎ বোঝানো when-clause-এ will বসে না, present simple: comes।

    উৎস: JnU B Unit 2010–11

  38. ৩৮. The police discovered that the suspect ____ before dawn.

    1. escapes
    2. has escaped
    3. had escaped
    4. will escape
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. had escaped

    The escape preceded the past discovery.

    পুলিশ জানার আগেই সে পালিয়েছিল—আগের অতীত কাজে past perfect: had escaped।

  39. ৩৯. She said she ____ working when the lights went out.

    1. is
    2. was
    3. will be
    4. has been
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. was

    Past reporting and a past ongoing action: was working.

    বাতি নিভে যাওয়ার সময় কাজ চলছিল—past continuous: was working।

  40. ৪০. If I ____ you, I would apologise.

    1. am
    2. was
    3. were
    4. will be
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. were

    Hypothetical if-clause conventionally uses were for all persons.

    অবাস্তব শর্তে (আমি তুমি নই) সব subject-এর সঙ্গে were: If I were you।

  41. ৪১. He will wait until she ____.

    1. will return
    2. returns
    3. returned
    4. would return
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. returns

    Until-clause referring to the future takes present simple.

    Until দিয়ে শুরু ভবিষ্যৎ clause-এ will বসে না, present simple: returns।

  42. ৪২. The teacher told us that we ____ hard.

    1. ought to work
    2. ought worked
    3. ought working
    4. ought to worked
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: A. ought to work

    Modal-like ought to is followed by base verb and remains unchanged in reported speech.

    Ought to-এর পরে base verb বসে এবং reported speech-এ এটি বদলায় না: ought to work।

  43. ৪৩. He said that he ____ help me the following day.

    1. can
    2. could
    3. will
    4. has
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. could

    Can commonly backshifts to could and tomorrow becomes the following day.

    Can → could এবং tomorrow → the following day: could help me the following day।

  44. ৪৪. When I arrived, they ____ lunch.

    1. have
    2. had
    3. were having
    4. will have
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. were having

    The lunch action was in progress at the time of arrival.

    আমি পৌঁছানোর সময় তারা lunch করছিল—চলমান অতীত কাজে past continuous: were having।

  45. ৪৫. I will go if he ____.

    1. will come
    2. comes
    3. came
    4. would come
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. comes

    First conditional uses present simple after if.

    First conditional: if-এর পরে present simple (comes), will নয়।

  46. ৪৬. He said that he ____ the book already.

    1. reads
    2. has read
    3. had read
    4. will read
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. had read

    Already completed before the past reporting time: past perfect.

    Already + বলার আগেই শেষ হওয়া কাজ → past perfect: had read।

  47. ৪৭. The teacher said that the exam ____ the next day.

    1. will be
    2. would be
    3. is being
    4. has been
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. would be

    A future event from a past viewpoint takes would.

    Said (past) থাকায় ভবিষ্যতের will হয় would; tomorrow হয় the next day।

  48. ৪৮. She asked me what I ____.

    1. am doing
    2. was doing
    3. do I do
    4. will do
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. was doing

    Reported question uses statement order and past backshift.

    Reported question-এ statement order থাকে (do I do নয়) এবং asked past হওয়ায় was doing।

  49. ৪৯. He will stay here until the rain ____.

    1. will stop
    2. stops
    3. stopped
    4. would stop
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. stops

    Until-clause about the future uses present simple.

    Until দিয়ে শুরু ভবিষ্যৎ clause-এ present simple: stops।

  50. ৫০. They said that they ____ the project the previous week.

    1. complete
    2. completed
    3. had completed
    4. will complete
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. had completed

    Previous week precedes the past reporting time: past perfect.

    আগের সপ্তাহে শেষ হয়েছিল, বলা হয়েছে পরে—আগের অতীতে past perfect: had completed।
