⚖️ Comparison (Degree) MCQ — উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যাসহ

Positive, Comparative, Superlative; as…as ও the more…the more · মোট ৯০টি প্রশ্ন। প্রতিটি প্রশ্নের নিচে “উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা” চাপলে সঠিক উত্তর আর বাংলা ব্যাখ্যা দেখাবে।

💡

দুজনের মধ্যে comparative (the better of the two), তিন বা বেশির মধ্যে superlative।

⏱️ Comparison (Degree) Model Test দাও 🎮 Fun Quiz

  1. ১. She wanted to have ____ of the two pieces of cake. 📌 CU-D 2024–25

    1. the smaller
    2. smallest
    3. the small
    4. more small
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: A. the smaller

    Of the two থাকায় comparative smaller; এর আগে the বসে।

  2. ২. My students’ sleepless nights became ____ as the finals approached. 📌 CU-B 2024–25; JnU-B 2016–17

    1. so frequently
    2. much more frequently
    3. as frequent
    4. more frequent
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: D. more frequent

    Became linking verb হওয়ায় adjective লাগে। More frequent adjective phrase; frequently adverb।

  3. ৩. Cuba is ____ sugar-growing countries in the world. 📌 CU-B1 2023–24

    1. one of the most largest
    2. one of largest
    3. one of the largest
    4. largest
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. one of the largest

    One of the + superlative + plural noun: one of the largest countries।

  4. ৪. Dhaka has become one of the ____ cities in Asia. 📌 CU-A 2023–24

    1. busy
    2. more busy
    3. busiest
    4. most busiest
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. busiest

    One of the-এর পরে superlative লাগে। Busy → busiest; most busiest double superlative।

  5. ৫. My research report is much bigger ____ my friend. 📌 CU-B 2022–23

    1. than
    2. then
    3. than that
    4. than that of
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: D. than that of

    Report-এর সঙ্গে friend নয়, friend-এর report তুলনা করতে হবে; তাই than that of।

  6. ৬. This chair is ____ the other. 📌 CU-B 2022–23

    1. comfortable than
    2. more comfortable than
    3. more comfortable then
    4. comfortably than
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. more comfortable than

    Comfortable-এর comparative more comfortable; তুলনার শব্দ than, then নয়।

  7. ৭. Jara has ____ records now as I had last year. 📌 DU-B 2018–19

    1. a half many as
    2. half as more
    3. half as many
    4. half as much as
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. half as many

    Records countable plural; multiple comparison: half as many + noun + as।

  8. ৮. This dictionary costs ____ the other one. 📌 DU-B 2018–19

    1. twice as more as
    2. twice as much as
    3. twice as much
    4. twice as many
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. twice as much as

    দামের তুলনায় অর্থের পরিমাণ বোঝাতে twice as much as ব্যবহৃত হয়।

  9. ৯. A comedy is ____ than an action movie. 📌 DU-B 2017–18

    1. more funnier
    2. funnier
    3. most funny
    4. funniest
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. funnier

    Funny-এর standard comparative funnier। Than এখানে comparative-এর clue।

  10. ১০. The longer I live here, ____. 📌 DU-D 2010–11

    1. I like the more
    2. the more I like it
    3. the more do I like it
    4. I like it more
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. the more I like it

    The + comparative-এর পরে normal subject–verb order: the more I like it।

  11. ১১. Which is ____ of the two? 📌 GST-A 2024–25

    1. more cheaper
    2. the cheap
    3. the cheapest
    4. the cheaper
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: D. the cheaper

    দুইটির মধ্যে নির্দিষ্ট একটি বাছাইয়ে the + comparative ব্যবহৃত হয়।

  12. ১২. ____ the matter gets, ____ he feels. 📌 JU-D 2024–25

    1. The easier, the better
    2. Easier, better
    3. The easiest, the best
    4. The easier, better
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: A. The easier, the better

    যত…তত বোঝাতে দুই অংশেই the + comparative লাগে: the easier…, the better…।

  13. ১৩. Which one is a superlative form? 📌 HSTU-D 2024–25

    1. less
    2. lesser
    3. least
    4. little
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. least

    Little → less → least; least হলো superlative।

  14. ১৪. The superlative form of “far” is ____. 📌 JU-C1 2023–24

    1. most far
    2. farther
    3. more far
    4. farthest
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: D. farthest

    Far → farther → farthest। Further/furthest-ও ব্যবহৃত হয়, তবে দেওয়া options-এ farthest সঠিক।

  15. ১৫. ____ higher you climb, ____ colder it gets. 📌 RU-B 2023–24

    1. The, the
    2. No article, no article
    3. The, a
    4. The, no article
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: A. The, the

    Correlative comparative-এর গঠন the + comparative…, the + comparative…।

  16. ১৬. Your answers are better than ____. 📌 JU-C 2023–24, 2022–23

    1. Mohan
    2. those Mohan
    3. those of Mohan
    4. that of Mohan
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. those of Mohan

    Answers plural; তাই একই জিনিসের তুলনায় those of Mohan ব্যবহার করতে হবে।

  17. ১৭. Choose the sentence that correctly compares the speed of his car with that of Mary’s car. 📌 ঢাবি ৭ কলেজ-Science 2023–24

    1. His car runs faster than Mary.
    2. His car runs faster than Mary’s.
    3. His car runs more than Mary.
    4. His car runs quickly than Mary’s.
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. His car runs faster than Mary’s.

    Mary’s এখানে Mary’s car বোঝায়। Car-এর সঙ্গে car-এর তুলনা হওয়ায় বাক্যটি logical।

  18. ১৮. His cooking is ____ than I remember, and also tastier! 📌 RU-B 2020–21

    1. most spicy
    2. more spicier
    3. spicier
    4. spicyer
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. spicier

    Consonant + y হলে y বদলে i হয়: spicy → spicier। More spicier ভুল।

  19. ১৯. Of the two girls, Sultana is the ____ intelligent. 📌 JKKNIU-AL 2018–19

    1. most
    2. more
    3. as
    4. far
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. more

    দুইজনের তুলনায় more intelligent; most intelligent নয়।

  20. ২০. In a technical sense, “speed” is not always ____ “velocity”. 📌 JnU-D 2016–17

    1. alike
    2. similar
    3. the same as
    4. as
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. the same as

    The same as পূর্ণ expression। Similar হলে to লাগত; alike-এর পরে এভাবে object বসে না।

  21. ২১. Choose the conventional positive-degree transformation of “He is one of the best boys in the village”. 📌 RU-E 2016–17

    1. No boy in the village is as good as he.
    2. Very few boys in the village are as good as he.
    3. Every boy in the village is better than he.
    4. He is better than every other boy in the village.
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. Very few boys in the village are as good as he.

    One of the best অর্থ অন্যতম সেরা; positive degree-তে very few…as good as ব্যবহার হয়।

  22. ২২. I feel ____ now. 📌 BU-B 2013–14

    1. more happier
    2. much happy
    3. much happier
    4. most happier
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. much happier

    Comparative happier-কে intensify করতে much বসে। More happier double comparative marking।

  23. ২৩. ____ more you read, ____ more you learn. 📌 DU-H 2024–25

    1. No article, the
    2. The, the
    3. The, no article
    4. No article, no article
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. The, the

    দুটি comparative একসাথে এলে The + comparative, the + comparative: The more…, the more…। তাই A, C ও D ভুল—দুই জায়গাতেই the লাগবে।

  24. ২৪. At the cafeteria Salina ate ____ sandwiches as Rubina. 📌 B Unit 2018–19

    1. three times as much as
    2. three times as many as
    3. three times as much
    4. three times as many
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: D. three times as many

    sandwiches গণনাযোগ্য, তাই many; পরে as আছে বলে three times as many। তাই three times as much as, three times as many as, three times as much এখানে ভুল।

  25. ২৫. It is the ____ city of the country. 📌 B Unit 2019–20

    1. most populous
    2. more populous
    3. most people
    4. most population
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: A. most populous

    populous (জনবহুল) adjective; the-এর পরে superlative most populous। তাই more populous, most people, most population এখানে ভুল।

  26. ২৬. His car runs faster than ____. 📌 DU 7 College Science 2023–24

    1. Mary
    2. Mary’s
    3. Mary runs
    4. Mary car
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. Mary’s

    গাড়ির সাথে গাড়ির তুলনা, তাই Mary’s (= Mary’s car)। তাই Mary, Mary runs, Mary car এখানে ভুল।

  27. ২৭. Which sentence is correct? 📌 D Unit 2016–17

    1. The more you read, less you understand.
    2. The more you read, the less you understand.
    3. More you read, the less you understand.
    4. The more you read, the less understand.
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. The more you read, the less you understand.

    দুই অংশেই the লাগে: The more…, the less…। তাই অন্য option (A, C, D) এই নিয়মে বসে না।

  28. ২৮. The greater the demand, ____. 📌 B Unit 2005–06

    1. higher the price
    2. the higher the price
    3. the high the price
    4. lower the price
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. the higher the price

    The greater…, the higher… — দুই অংশেই the + comparative। তাই higher the price, the high the price, lower the price এখানে ভুল।

  29. ২৯. Which is the correct sentence?

    1. The Nile is longest river in Africa.
    2. Nile is longest river in the Africa.
    3. The Nile is the longest river in Africa.
    4. Nile is longest river in Africa.
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. The Nile is the longest river in Africa.

    নদীর নামের আগে the, superlative-এর আগেও the: The Nile is the longest river। তাই অন্য option (A, B, D) এই নিয়মে বসে না।

  30. ৩০. There is plenty of food. You can have ____ you like.

    1. as much as
    2. many more
    3. much
    4. much more
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: A. as much as

    food uncountable, তাই as much as (যত খুশি)। তাই many more, much, much more এখানে ভুল।

  31. ৩১. The clearer your thought is, the ____ your writing becomes.

    1. good
    2. worse
    3. better
    4. best
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. better

    The clearer…, the better… — good-এর comparative better। good positive, best superlative, আর worse অর্থে উল্টো; তাই এগুলো ভুল।

  32. ৩২. ____ 1000 species of fish have been identified.

    1. As many
    2. As many as
    3. As much as
    4. Much as
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. As many as

    সংখ্যার আগে জোর দিতে as many as। তাই As many, As much as, Much as এখানে ভুল।

  33. ৩৩. The water of the Great Salt Lake is ____ sea water.

    1. saltier than that of
    2. as salty as that of
    3. saltier than
    4. so salty as
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. saltier than

    লেকের পানির সাথে সাগরের পানির তুলনা, তাই saltier than sea water। তাই saltier than that of, as salty as that of, so salty as এখানে ভুল।

  34. ৩৪. Mr. Hussain usually earns ____ his brother.

    1. twice as much as
    2. twice more than
    3. twice as many as
    4. twice as more as
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: A. twice as much as

    আয় বা টাকা uncountable, তাই twice as much as। তাই twice more than, twice as many as, twice as more as এখানে ভুল।

  35. ৩৫. ‘This is one of the easiest subjects.’ The comparative form is ____.

    1. Very few subjects are so easy as this.
    2. This is easier than most other subjects.
    3. This is more easier subject.
    4. All of the above.
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. This is easier than most other subjects.

    one of the easiest = easier than most other subjects। তাই অন্য option (A, C, D) এই নিয়মে বসে না।

  36. ৩৬. Select the correct option.

    1. Your answers are better than Mr. Paper.
    2. Your answers are better than Mr. Paper answers.
    3. Your answers are better than those of Mr. Paper.
    4. Your answers are better than answered by Mr. Paper.
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. Your answers are better than those of Mr. Paper.

    উত্তরের সাথে উত্তরের তুলনা করতে হয়: those of Mr. Paper। A-তে answers-এর সাথে মানুষের তুলনা, B-তে possessive (’s) নেই, D-এর গঠন ভুল।

  37. ৩৭. This is the ____ version.

    1. new
    2. newer
    3. latest
    4. none
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. latest

    সবচেয়ে নতুন সংস্করণ বোঝাতে latest। তাই new, newer, none এখানে ভুল।

  38. ৩৮. Jabed’s luck couldn’t have been ____ than Abed’s.

    1. worst
    2. worse
    3. worser
    4. worsted
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. worse

    than-এর আগে comparative; bad-এর comparative worse (worser ভুল)। তাই worst, worser, worsted এখানে ভুল।

  39. ৩৯. The richer he gets, ____.

    1. the more friends he has
    2. more friends he has
    3. more friends has he
    4. more and more friends he has
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: A. the more friends he has

    The richer…, the more friends… — দুই অংশে the। তাই more friends he has, more friends has he, more and more friends he has এখানে ভুল।

  40. ৪০. The more she worked, ____.

    1. she achieved much
    2. she achieved the less
    3. the less she achieved
    4. the much achieved she
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. the less she achieved

    The more…, the less… — দ্বিতীয় অংশেও the + comparative। তাই she achieved much, she achieved the less, the much achieved she এখানে ভুল।

  41. ৪১. His brother is ____ he looks.

    1. younger than
    2. young as
    3. younger
    4. very young
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: A. younger than

    তুলনায় comparative + than: younger than। তাই young as, younger, very young এখানে ভুল।

  42. ৪২. What is the superlative degree of ‘costly’?

    1. costier
    2. costly
    3. costliest
    4. costlyest
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. costliest

    consonant + y হলে y → i করে -est: costliest। তাই costier, costly, costlyest এখানে ভুল।

  43. ৪৩. For better or ____, computer is a very important device.

    1. good
    2. sad
    3. well
    4. worse
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: D. worse

    নির্দিষ্ট phrase: for better or worse (ভালো হোক বা মন্দ)। তাই good, sad, well এখানে ভুল।

  44. ৪৪. Choose the grammatically incorrect sentence.

    1. Afzal is my elder brother.
    2. He is the oldest man of this village.
    3. He is two years younger to me.
    4. Rajib is five years older than me.
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: C. He is two years younger to me.

    younger-এর পরে than বসে, to নয় (senior/junior-এর পরে to)। তাই অন্য option (A, B, D) এই নিয়মে বসে না।

  45. ৪৫. Choose the correct comparative of ‘A string of pearls was not so bright as her teeth.’

    1. A string of pearls were very bright than her teeth.
    2. Her teeth were brighter than a string of pearls.
    3. A string of pearls was brighter than her teeth.
    4. Her teeth was more brighter than a string of pearls.
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. Her teeth were brighter than a string of pearls.

    not so bright as উল্টে লিখলে: Her teeth were brighter than…। তাই অন্য option (A, C, D) এই নিয়মে বসে না।

  46. ৪৬. What is the superlative degree of “bad”? 📌 JUST B Unit 2017–18

    1. baddest
    2. worst
    3. worse
    4. good
    উত্তর ও ব্যাখ্যা

    উত্তর: B. worst

    bad → worse → worst। তাই baddest, worse, good এখানে ভুল।

  47. ৪৭. Which of the following is comparative? 📌 PUST 2008–09

    1. less
    2. worst
    3. happy
    4. least
